# WebBlaze — our own brand identity

**This is WebBlaze's identity for OUR OWN materials** (outreach console, WebBlaze.io site,
email signatures, decks). It is deliberately different from every client's look.

> Rule: CLIENT previews/sites match the CLIENT's brand (Sun = navy+gold, W Employment = teal).
> WebBlaze's own materials use THIS. Never dress WebBlaze in a client's colors. If a prospect
> asks for a portfolio, show them the client work (Sun, WEL) — that's the proof, not our template.

## Personality
Fast, modern, plainspoken, confident. A young studio that makes small local businesses look
sharp and current. Friendly, zero jargon, no corporate stiffness.

## Color
- `--ink:    #16130F`  warm near-black (text, dark surfaces)
- `--blaze:  #F14E23`  signature ember-orange — the "blaze". Primary accent + primary buttons.
- `--blaze-d:#D63E16`  hover/darker
- `--ember:  #FF8A3D`  lighter orange for gradients/highlights (use sparingly)
- `--paper:  #FAFAF9`  neutral near-white ground (NOT cream)
- `--surface:#FFFFFF`  cards
- `--muted:  #726B62`  secondary text
- `--line:   #ECEAE6`  hairlines
One bold accent (blaze), everything else quiet. Orange is the only saturated color.

## Type
- Display / wordmark: **Sora** 700–800 (geometric, modern). Google Fonts.
- Body / UI: **Hanken Grotesk** 400–700. Google Fonts.
- (No serifs — that's client territory. No Inter/Space Grotesk — overused.)

## Wordmark
"WebBlaze" — "Web" in ink, "Blaze" in blaze-orange, Sora 800, tight tracking. Optional spark/
flame glyph before it. Keep it simple and confident.

## Tagline options
- "Modern websites for local businesses."
- "Your business, online and looking sharp."

## Don't
- Don't reuse Sun's navy+gold or WEL's teal for WebBlaze.
- Don't use the AI-cliché looks (cream + terracotta + serif, near-black + acid green,
  purple→blue gradient hero, everything centered/rounded).
