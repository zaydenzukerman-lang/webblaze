## Pricing model (UPDATED 2026-09-04 per Zayden)
- **Website + hosting = ONE bundled YEARLY charge.** Includes the build, hosting, unlimited
  updates, daily backups, weekly reports, initial SEO. Do NOT split build fee vs service anymore.
- **We were charging too little** ($300/yr). Raise it. Still anchor + discount:
  **LOCKED default: "normally $949/year, for you $629/year"** (Zayden approved 2026-09-04, matches
  what Forest sent Jacob). Adjust per client but this is the standard.
  **IMPORTANT: $629 is the REAL and ONLY price everyone pays. $949 is a permanent fake anchor —
  EVERY prospect always gets the "$629 discount." Nobody ever pays $949.** The discount just makes
  the buyer feel special. So: never frame the discount as limited-time / expiring / for-them-only
  in a way that could be checked; keep it as a simple "normally $949, yours is $629."
- **Google Maps / local SEO = MONTHLY, $500/mo** — the money-maker, pitched to everyone. Separate
  from the website yearly.
- **No lock-in**: cancel anytime, keep the site, move servers. Kills the risk objection.

## What makes the email convert (copy these moves)
1. Warm, contextual opener ("As I previously mentioned my son Zayden...").
2. Premium positioning: "high-end modern websites," "latest technology," "full ownership."
3. SPECIFIC benefit bullets, not vague ones. Real examples he used:
   - Full ownership, built on the latest technology
   - Faster load times, better mobile, a funnel built to convert visitors to contact
   - Spam + malware protection, 1-minute uptime pinging
   - Unlimited updates completed within 24 hours (photos, landing pages, promo pages)
   - Daily backups, restore within 4 hours
   - Weekly Google Analytics reports
   - Full initial SEO optimization for navigation + Google visibility
4. Show the live proof (link to the built site).
5. Human, plain, no em-dashes, minimal punctuation — still Zayden's rules.
