## How Maps ranking works
Three factors: **relevance** (how well you match the search), **distance** (can't change it),
**prominence** (how known/trusted you are). We influence relevance + prominence.
2026 factor weights: **Google Business Profile 32%**, on-page 19%, **reviews 16%**, links 15%,
behavioral 8%, citations 7%. Top individual factors: **primary category #1, reviews #2,**
complete profile, and NAP consistency.

## The legit levers (our monthly checklist per client)
1. **Claim & verify** the Google Business Profile (GBP). Unlocks everything.
2. **Categories** — pick the most specific PRIMARY category (strongest relevance signal), then add
   several accurate secondary categories (more categories = higher average rank).
3. **100% complete profile** (7x more clicks): hours, services, products, full description,
   attributes, service area, website link, Q&A.
4. **NAP consistency** — Name/Address/Phone identical on GBP, the website, and every directory.
   Even tiny mismatches suppress ranking.
5. **Reviews** — the #2 factor. Set up a simple system to ask EVERY customer for a review (see
   ethics below). Target 4.5+ stars and respond to every review, good or bad.
6. **Weekly Google Posts** — offers, project photos, updates. Fresh activity signals.
7. **Photos** — upload new ones regularly (jobs, storefront, team).
8. **Citations** — get listed consistently in local/industry directories (feeds NAP).
9. **Website on-page** — location + service keywords, a page per service/city, local schema. This
   is where WebBlaze's website work directly boosts the Maps ranking.

## ⚠️ ETHICS — the thing that can destroy this business (read twice)
Reviews are legally policed. NEVER:
- Buy or fake reviews. Post reviews yourself or via staff ("insider" reviews). Offer incentives
  for reviews. Set review quotas.
- **Review-gate** = ask how it went, then send only happy customers to Google and route unhappy
  ones elsewhere. This is BANNED by the FTC and Google.
FTC fines run up to ~$51,744 PER violation; a retailer was fined $4.2M for blocking bad reviews.
LEGIT = ask ALL customers with the SAME public review link, no incentives, never script what they
say. Genuinely improving service + asking everyone is the whole game. (Matches our no-fake-reviews rule.)
