How It Works

Big premiums, made manageable.

Instead of paying a large insurance premium in one lump sum, let us finance it — you keep your cash working and your coverage in force.

01

Secure your policy

Choose the coverage you need — commercial, property, life or specialty insurance — through your agent.

02

We pay the premium

Sun Premium Financing pays your insurer the full annual premium up front, so your policy is fully in force.

03

You repay in installments

Repay us in manageable scheduled payments over the policy term — no large lump sum out of pocket.

04

Keep your cash working

Your working capital stays free for payroll, inventory and growth, instead of locked up in prepaid insurance.
