About Us

The law offices of Robert D. Schwartz

Established In 1996 with the goal of helping clients with their estate planning needs.

Over the years, Robert has prepared more than ten thousand revocable trusts for his clients and has gathered a great deal of experience in affordable estate planning. Through his hard work and dedication to his clients, Robert has been able to grow the practice and add several other attorneys to the staff, each bringing their own expertise and specialties. The Law Offices of Robert D. Schwartz has now grown to include several office locations throughout the state of Florida and is able to accommodate clients in many different areas of practice. We offer services in elder law, as well.

Our attorneys strive to provide the highest quality personalized legal representation, and we are committed to clear and consistent communication with our clients. We understand client satisfaction is of the utmost importance, and we work hard to earn your trust and confidence. If you have any questions, comments or legal issues that you would like to discuss, please do not hesitate to contact us. Thank you for considering us; we are very excited to work with you.
