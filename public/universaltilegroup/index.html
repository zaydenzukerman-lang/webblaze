Who we are

Family owned since 1972

Family owned and operated since 1972, Universal Tile Group prides itself as a direct importer of high quality tile at competitive pricing. The company began as a small showroom in Miami Beach as the first American venture of founder Hugo Ignorato. In the mid-1980s, Hugo’s children — Gaetano, Luigi, and Angelica — carried on their father’s legacy by expanding into installations and home renovations.
