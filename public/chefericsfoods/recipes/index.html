BBQ Wings With a Difference

Ingredients

  • 1 LB Chicken Wings
  • 1/2 Tbs Chef Eric's Best BBQ Jerk Sauce
  • (Also 3/4 Cup Chef Eric's Best BBQ Jerk Sauce)
  • 1/4 Cup Pineapple Juice
  • Salt to taste (a pinch of salt)

Directions

  1. Heat pot on medium to high heat with enough oil to cover Chicken Wings.
  2. Marinade Chicken Wings with 1/2 tablespoon Chef Eric's Best BBQ Jerk Sauce and Salt.
  3. Then deep fry for 3 minutes.
  4. Next remove chicken wings from oil and place in a baking dish, mix Pineapple juice and 3/4 cup BBQ Sauce together, pour it on chicken wings and finish it in the oven at 350°F for 20-30 minutes.
  5. Ready To Serve.

Jerk Touch-Up Shrimp With Mango

Ingredients

  • 12oz Shrimp (Medium)
  • 1/2 Tbs Chef Eric's Best Jerk Touch-Up Sauce
  • 1/2 Tbs Chopped Garlic
  • 1 Sprig of Thyme (Cut up)
  • 1/2 Cup Diced Mango
  • 1 Tbs Butter
  • 1 Tbs oil

Directions

  1. Heat oil and butter on medium heat, combine all the other ingredients (except mango) including Chef Eric's Best Jerk Touch-Up and saute for 3 minutes.
  2. Then add mango and saute for another 3-4 minutes.
  3. Ready To Serve.

Red Wine Jerk Salmon

Ingredients

  • 2 Slices 8oz Salmon or Fish Fillet of choice
  • 2 Tbsp Chef Erics Best Red Wine Jerk Seasoning
  • 1 Tbsp Olive Oil

Directions

  1. Combine Chef Erics Best Red Wine Jerk Seasoning and Olive Oil in a bowl.
  2. Apply mixture to Fish, heat skillet on medium to high heat, then add the fish and let cook for 7-10 minutes on each side.
  3. Serve with your favorite vegetables or with rice.

Recipe

Jerk Chicken & Vegetables Stir Fry

Ingredients

  • 1 Lb Boneless Chicken Breast or Thigh
  • 2 Tbsp Chef Erics Best Jerk Seasoning
  • 2 Cups Vegetable (of choice)
  • 2 Tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 Tbsp Butter

Directions

  1. Add Chef Erics Best Jerk Seasoning to Chicken and let sit.
  2. Meanwhile place Skillet on stove, add Olive Oil and Butter and let heat on medium to high heat, then add Chicken and saute for 10 minutes.
  3. Then add vegetables and let simmer for another 5-10 minutes (covered).
  4. Ready to serve.
