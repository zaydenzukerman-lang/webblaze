Air purifiers & UV lights

Not sure what type of air purifier is right for you?

Our knowledgeable technicians can help you choose the option that best fits your home's needs and your indoor air quality goals — RGF Guardian Air REME-HALO or a BLU QR UV light.

RGF Guardian Air REME-HALO

Designed to treat the air throughout your home, the REME-HALO purifies every cubic foot of air at the same time. These units don't need pollutants to travel through the ductwork to be neutralized — every time your fan blows air, it carries purifying oxidizers into the conditioned space, attacking bacteria, viruses and mold spores at the source and reducing odors.

The REME-HALO is installed as part of your home's central heating and cooling system, completely out of your way and out of sight.

Can help reduce

  • Smoke odors
  • Cooking odors
  • Pet odors
  • Trash odors
  • "Musty" or stale air smells
  • Paint & chemical odors

BLU QR® UV Stick Lite

The BLU QR UV Stick Lite uses the natural power of ultraviolet light to control airborne allergens and microorganisms — bacteria, viruses and mold growth on HVAC interior surfaces — keeping your coils cleaner and helping maintain system performance.

  • Exclusive quick-release lamp replacement
  • Four mounting options
  • 24 VAC power supply, mounts in HVAC system
  • Provides UV light disinfection to AC coil surfaces from mold, bacteria, virus and odors
