Team Cooking Parties

How does it work?

Bachelorette parties, corporate executives, singles or any other group work collectively and bond in a fun, unusual setting.

All of the participants work towards a common goal: creating a four-star gourmet dinner from scratch and then enjoying the results!

Upon arrival, all guests are placed in teams and team names are chosen. Our chefs will then give a demonstration of the dishes, along with the culture and history of the selected menu. Guests will then go to their designated stations with the guidance of individual instructors to recreate the dishes. Friendly competition among the teams is encouraged and prizes are awarded at the end of the day.

Private cooking classes

Most classes average $25 to $45 per person with a five person minimum and up to 20 students per class.

Classes can be held in your home or in our kitchen; I prefer to teach people in a kitchen where they are going to be recreating these dishes and culinary procedures.

Often we cook a full dinner and then sit down and share our creations with friends and family… what I call the “Supper Club”.

Preparing for culinary school

Professional tutoring: $20 an hour.

I can help you gain advanced placement when entering culinary schools like Johnson & Wales, saving you thousands of dollars in tuition fees.

Mentoring to my students after classes and helping them with future culinary questions that arise is free to all our students. Tutoring and mentoring culinary students can make a huge difference in placement after graduation and accelerated ability to move up the brigade in a professional kitchen.
