Spanish Paella Parties

Birthdays are celebrated, patron saints honored and Sunday outings made complete by the famous rice dish. Many call it paellera — the term comes from the Latin patella, a flat plate on which offerings were made to the gods. By the 16th century the Castilian word paella was being used for a vessel similar to the paella pan.

  • Paella ValencianaTraditional Paella with mussels, clams, calamari, shrimp, scallops, Chorizo sausage and roasted chicken cooked in aromatic saffron rice.
  • Paella RupestreA country, meat-only Paella prepared with Morcilla & Bilbao sausage, pork tenderloin, roasted chicken and lamb.
  • Paella MarineraA Paella for seafood lovers, with lobster, mussels, clams, calamari, shrimp and scallops cooked in aromatic saffron rice.
  • Paella VegetarianaWith grilled seasoned asparagus, zucchini, yellow squash, sweet red and yellow bell peppers, eggplant, carrots and Portobello mushroom over saffron rice.
Vegetarian and special diets: We can often cook single or a few portions for those with special diet requirements alongside a main meal at no additional cost. Our Paella Pescado and Verduras are both gluten free.
