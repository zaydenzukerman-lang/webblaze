More Hors d’Oeuvres

Simple foods, creatively presented, comforts for the soul. The following hors d’oeuvres may be tray passed or served buffet style. Remember we make everything from scratch and design our menus specifically for the customer and function. These are ideas, “food for thought”; anything can be created for you.
