BBQ, Pig Pickin’, Picnic and Just Havin’ Fun

Barbeque cooked or smoked on the grill or pit. Meat pulled, chopped, or carved and flavored to your decided style of sauces, brines, marinades or rubs… we can do it all no matter where you grew up or what style of BBQ you grew up on. If you don’t know, we will give you some insight into how it’s been done.

Planning a whole-animal roast? For specialty whole meat it is better to give two months’ notice so the farmer can select and feed the animal.
