Our story

Our knowledge is our Product

Since 1946, Lewis Electric has always strived to improve the knowledge of our employees to the benefit of not only the Company, but also our Valued Customers. Our knowledge is our Product and we are on the leading edge of our field. Doing the job right the first time saves everyone money.

In the time we have been in business there have only been three Master Electricians in charge. All three have been in the forefront of the technology available in their time.

In 1996, the Company obtained its Florida State Certified Electrical Contractor License. We are now licensed in all of Florida's 67 Counties. Our experience and expertise is now available for installations State-wide and our consulting services and Control Systems available World-wide.

We can be of help in your next project. Be it Construction, Renovation, Maintenance Surveys, Consulting, or just a little question answered.

E.T. Samples
President, CEO & Qualifier, I.L.S. Enterprises Inc.

Hurricane Andrew, 1992

Open the day after the storm

Lewis Electric re-opened the day after Hurricane Andrew hit. One of our secretaries brought an 8 kW generator from Tampa. We found all of our phone lines in a pile down the street and ran lines on anything that would hold them up. Power and Phone!

For the next two weeks our crews were hooking up generators, clearing shorts, and working on their own homes at night. When the storm hit we had 4 Crews. All through the rebuilding we still had 4 Crews. It was the only way to maintain quality control. Each crew grew in size but was still under the supervision of one of our Journeymen.

"I am very Proud of my personnel and the way they conducted themselves in that darkest of times. The compassion and goodness of heart of all concerned got all of us through it." – E.T. Samples
