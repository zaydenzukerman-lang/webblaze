Power Costs

Zero Demand Systems

In certain parts of the country, (Florida in particular) Power Companies without excess generation capability will offer their Customers the option of a Program generally called "Zero Demand". This program provides incentives to high demand customers willing to shed their load during Peak usage periods. The savings can be substantial.

We can give you a complete Analysis of your Plant Electrical usage, Expected savings on your Power Bill and Design and price a system to meet your needs. Plant Size is Unlimited.

  • Lower Power Costs.
  • Automatic Plant backup in case of Power Failure.
  • Critical loads can be served – for processes that would be financially detrimental to the operation if power were lost (example: certification of produce shipped to another state that has to be kept at a constant temperature for a defined period).

Example installation

A 4,200 Ampere, 277/480 Volt Electrical Service consisting of (4) 800 Ampere Mains and (2) 600 Ampere Mains feeding a large Lime and Avocado processing plant in the South Florida Area, fed on the Power Company side by a 1500 KVA Pad Mount Transformer. All of the loads are transferred to the Generator using Lakeshore Automatic Transfer Switches (other brands are available).

When a signal is received via phone line from the Power Company to the interface, the Generator starts and the Transfer is initiated. At the end of the Peak cycle, a signal to return to Main power is received, the loads transfer and the generator runs at no load for a predetermined time for cooling purposes before shutdown. The customer expected full payout of this installation in less than 4 years.

Lighting

Parking Lot Lighting Surveys & Lighting Upgrades

The object of a lighting survey is to determine the footcandles on the parking surface. There are a few reasons for this type of survey:

  • To check for compliance with a local ordinance.
  • To determine if the footcandle level is compatible with the recommended levels for the specific use of the area.
  • To see how much the existing lighting has degraded over time. It takes the same amount of power to run a fixture that is operating at 100% efficiency as it does for one operating at 50%. You just have half the light.

Lewis Electric will provide a scaled rendering of your parking area complete with point by point measurements of lighting levels in either lumens or footcandles. As an option, the existing fixture photometric data with tilt and orientation can be overlaid.

Lewis Electric will also provide site appraisals, recommendations, quotes and plans for compliance with parking lot and alley lighting requirements (Dade County Ordinance 96-171). We can design outdoor lighting with complete photometric data to find the most efficient and economical way to comply.
