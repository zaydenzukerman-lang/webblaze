Custom Machinery Control Panels – Growers Packing Company, Florida City

Complete stand alone units comprising all components needed to operate processing machinery. This unit controls a complete Tomato Packing Facility – (135) Motors.

  • Flexibility – Stand alone units can be placed anywhere. Make troubleshooting easier by increased access to all components.
  • Phase Failure and Undervoltage Protection – All motors protected. No more motors lost due to single phasing.
  • Sequence Start – Motors start in reverse sequence of product flow in a timed pattern. The startup current of an electric motor is approx 600% of full load current, so the control ensures an even startup. If one motor overloads, every motor feeding product to it shuts down. No more product pileups.

TVSS

Surge & Transient Voltage Protection

Lewis Electric is a Dealer of Innovative Technologies Inc. products and also utilizes Citel Inc. Surge Protectors. 10 Year warranty on most units, 5 Year on others (No Hassle). Full Line – from the Largest Electrical Services to low Voltage, Telephone and Data circuits. Export and installation available.

What is a transient?

  • Fast-rising electrical disturbance
  • Brief in duration
  • Repeated transients generated internally by load switching, load cycling, load faults
  • Random transients generated externally by lightning and load switching by neighboring facilities

How they affect you

  • Cumulative damage: erratic equipment behavior, scrambled or lost data, component degradation
  • Catastrophic damage: immediate equipment failure, downtime
  • Expensive repairs, lost productivity, reduced profits

How protection helps

  • Prevents surges from damaging equipment
  • Reduces maintenance costs
  • Eliminates downtime due to transient activity
  • Increases operational efficiency

If you feel that you might have Surge / Transient problems (almost everyone does) please feel free to contact us regarding your needs: power, data or telephone.
