ABOUT Sun Finance Company

Sun Finance Co. LLC came into existence over 60 years ago, in 1958. While much has changed over the years for Sun Finance Co. LLC many things remain the same. We now have new locations and some new faces, but we still provide personal loans of various sizes and we service our own personal loans and mortgages in house. When you do business with Sun Finance Co. LLC you can be confident you are working with a knowledgeable and established company, and you can have the piece of mind of knowing Sun Finance Co. LLC will be here tomorrow just like we are today.
