Common questions

Common Questions

How do I estimate income for the Marketplace when it changes every month?

Use your best estimate of net self-employment income for the year and update it if things change. Updating mid-year helps avoid owing money back when you file taxes.

Can I deduct my health insurance premiums?

Many self-employed people can deduct premiums for themselves and their family, as long as they weren't eligible for an employer-subsidized plan. Your tax professional can confirm whether you qualify.

I just went out on my own. When can I enroll?

Losing job-based coverage opens a 60-day Special Enrollment Period for Marketplace plans, so you don't have to wait for Open Enrollment. If you have COBRA, compare it before the election window closes.

Do I need a business to get coverage?

No. Self-employed people usually buy individual or family coverage. A small-group plan is generally only an option once your business has eligible employees.

Do you only sell one company's plans?

No — we're an independent brokerage and compare plans across multiple carriers, including regional Blue-affiliated plans, national PPO carriers, integrated HMO carriers, and ACA Marketplace-focused carriers, so the recommendation is based on fit, not a quota.

Is this service actually free?

Yes. Licensed brokers are compensated by the insurance carrier at enrollment, not by you, so comparing plans and enrolling through us costs nothing extra.

How long does a consultation take?

Most first calls run 20-30 minutes. We'll ask about your household, budget, and current coverage, then walk through 2-3 plan options that fit — no pressure to decide on the spot.
