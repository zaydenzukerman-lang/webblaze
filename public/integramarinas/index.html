The marina

High-end dockage inside a 2,000-resident island community.

Located in Aventura, Florida, midway between Miami and Fort Lauderdale, Williams Island Marina is part of the greater master-planned Williams Island community of over 2,000 residents. Marina boaters can access indoor and outdoor pools, fitness centers, tennis courts, and food & beverage options.

  • Fixed and floating concrete docks
  • Vessels up to 140′ in length, 10′ max draft
  • Fuel on site, plus a Fuel Concierge
  • Deep-water access from the Intracoastal Waterway
