Wellness Visits

Preventative care is the heart of a long, healthy life. Whether it’s a brand-new puppy or kitten, an adult in their prime, or a senior who needs a closer eye, our wellness visits are built around your pet’s age and lifestyle.

Book This VisitCall (305) 653-3939

Wellness care includes

  • New Puppy and Kitten exams
  • Adult and Senior Wellness
  • Immunizations / Vaccines
  • Microchipping
  • Parasite control
  • Preventative Medicine
  • Weight and Nutrition Management
  • Behavioral Management

What to expect

01

A thorough exam

Dr. Ahuja examines your pet nose to tail and takes the time to hear what you’ve noticed at home.

02

A plan for their stage of life

Vaccines, parasite prevention, weight and nutrition, and behavior — tailored to your pet, not a template.

03

Clear next steps

You leave knowing exactly what your pet needs now and what to watch for before the next visit.
