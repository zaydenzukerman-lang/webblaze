Trust AAA Pool & Spa Service, Inc for servicing and cleaning your pool.

Our story

Excellence, systematized for every pool

Through the years we have systematized excellence requirements for each type of pool according to its needs, offering a personal service that has allowed us to build an unrivaled reputation in terms of customer satisfaction.

Thank you for choosing AAA Pool & Spa Service, Inc. for all your pool and spa needs in recent years and many years to come. In an effort to stay ahead of technology, we attend many training seminars and product information sessions throughout the year. This allows us to offer industry-leading products, and we assure you the best experience with our company.

This year focuses on green technology and ways to save money with new pool equipment and cleaning equipment.

Our pool technicians are CPO, Certified Pool and Spa Operators, by the National Swimming Pool Foundation (NSPF).

Pool & spa cleaning and maintenance since 2000.

Our staff

The people who take care of your pool

Our own employees

Our maintenance technicians are employed by us. They are not subcontractors.

Background checked

Before being hired, our employees submit to a criminal background check and driving record check. They are trained to work on the job site and they must demonstrate skills and knowledge.

Certified

Our pool technicians are CPO, Certified Pool and Spa Operators, by the National Swimming Pool Foundation (NSPF) and Certified Public Pool Service Technicians by the Miami Dade Health Department.

GPS-tracked trucks

All our company trucks are equipped with a GPS Tracking system to ensure your pool was visited and the time of the visit. This report is available to the customer at the time they request it.

Insured & bonded

Our employees are under worker's compensation insurance; our company has General Liability insurance and a Dishonesty Bond for your peace of mind.
