Our story

Independently owned, and proud of it.

Animal Clinic of Ives Dairy Road is locally owned and operated — not a corporate chain. That means unhurried visits, doctors who take the time to explain every step, and a team that remembers your pet by name and checks in when they've been under the weather.

From your very first phone call to the moment you walk out the door, we want you and your pet to feel at home. Because to us, that's exactly what you are — family.

Who you'll meet

Led by Dr. Aditi Ahuja, DVM.

Owner & Lead Veterinarian · Cornell DVM · Fear Free Certified

Dr. Aditi Ahuja earned her Doctorate of Veterinary Medicine from Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine, where she focused on Small Animal Medicine and was inducted into the Phi Zeta Veterinary Honor Society. She is Fear Free Certified and an active member of the AVMA and the Veterinary Business Management Association (VBMA).

She completed a rotating internship at Friendship Hospital for Animals in Washington, D.C., and has a passion for diagnosing and managing chronic diseases — guiding families through early diagnosis, intervention and lifelong care.
