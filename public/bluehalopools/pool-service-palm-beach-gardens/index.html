Pools in Palm Beach Gardens

More equipment than anywhere else in the county

Gardens pools tend to be bigger, more likely to have an attached spa, and far more likely to be running full automation. Variable-speed pumps, heaters, salt systems, water features and a controller tying it all together.

That is a different service call. More equipment means more that can drift out of adjustment and more that can fail expensively, and a controller will happily report a schedule running perfectly while the salt cell behind it has stopped producing. We check what the equipment is actually doing rather than what the panel claims, and it goes on the report with the chemistry.

Local know-how

What Gardens pools need

1

Automation that gets checked

A controller is only as good as the last time someone verified it against reality. We check output and pressure against the panel, not instead of it.

2

Pool and spa together

An attached spa is a second body of water with its own chemistry and its own heater. It should be quoted as such, not folded in as a rounding error.

3

Gate and community access

Most of these communities have gate procedures, approved-vendor lists and service hours. Give us the requirements at the quote and we work to them from the first visit.

Quoted on your pool

Gardens pools sit at the higher end of our range, and honestly so: they are larger, they carry more equipment, and most of them include a spa.

Weekly plans start at $129 a month, but a Mirasol or BallenIsles pool with a spa and automation is usually a Halo Complete or Signature conversation. We will tell you which at the quote.

Pool service in Palm Beach Gardens

Questions

Do you service pools in the gated communities?

Yes. PGA National, BallenIsles, Mirasol, Frenchman's and Evergrene among others. Most require gate procedures or an approved-vendor registration, so tell us at the quote and we handle it before the first visit.

Our pool has a spa and full automation. Is that a problem?

No, it is most of what Gardens pools look like. It does change the price, because a spa is a second body of water and automation needs verifying rather than trusting.

How much is pool service in Palm Beach Gardens?

Plans start at $129 a month, and every account is subject to a price quote by a technician or at the point of sale. Larger pools with spas and automation sit higher.
