Pools in Lantana

Two housing stocks, one small town

Lantana still has a good number of small mid-century pools behind original cottages, and alongside them a set of substantially larger waterfront properties. The two need different things and it would be easy to treat them the same.

The waterfront pools deal with salt and wind off the Intracoastal, which pushes chemistry around between visits and works on the equipment. The older inland cottages have smaller pools with older surfaces, where the honest conversation is usually about how much life is left in the plaster. We quote both on what they are rather than on the zip code they share.

Pool service in Lantana

Questions

Do you service the waterfront properties?

Yes. Those pools take salt and wind off the Intracoastal, so they get tighter testing and a weekly look at the equipment pad.

My pool is small and quite old. Is it worth putting on a plan?

Usually, yes. What we will be straight about is whether the surface has reached the point where resurfacing rather than chemistry is the real answer.

How much is pool service in Lantana?

Plans start at $129 a month for weekly service on a standard residential pool, and every account is subject to a price quote by a technician or at the point of sale.
