Pools in Jupiter

Well water changes everything

Jupiter Farms and the properties west of the turnpike are largely on well water, and well water carries iron and other metals. Top a pool off with it and you get staining on the surface, discoloration in the water, and chemistry that behaves in ways a coastal pool never does.

It is a solvable problem, but only if someone is actually testing for metals and sequestering rather than throwing more chlorine at brown water and making the staining worse. Nearer the coast the pools are newer, the equipment is modern, and the job looks more like Palm Beach Gardens. We treat the two ends of Jupiter as the different jobs they are.

Local know-how

What Jupiter pools need

1

Metals, out in the Farms

Iron from well water stains surfaces and turns water off-color. It needs testing for and sequestering, not more chlorine, which sets the stain rather than clearing it.

2

Large lots and heavy debris

Farms properties sit on acreage with real tree cover. Baskets fill fast and the organic load draws down chlorine between visits.

3

Newer equipment near the coast

Abacoa and the waterfront communities run modern variable-speed pumps and automation. Different job, same weekly report.

Pricing in Jupiter

Quoted on your pool

Pool service in Jupiter

Questions

We are on well water in Jupiter Farms. Can you handle the staining?

Yes, and it is the main thing that makes a Farms pool different. Metals need testing for and sequestering. Adding more chlorine to iron-stained water sets the stain rather than clearing it.

Do you cover Abacoa and the waterfront communities?

Yes. Those pools are newer and usually run modern equipment and automation, which we verify against what it is actually doing rather than what the panel reports.

How much is pool service in Jupiter?

Plans start at $129 a month, and every account is subject to a price quote by a technician or at the point of sale. Well-water properties usually sit a little higher.
