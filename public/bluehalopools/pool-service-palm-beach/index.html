Pools in Palm Beach

The standard is the point

Palm Beach pools are held to a standard that has nothing to do with chemistry ranges. The water is expected to look right every day, not on average, and the town itself sets rules about service hours, vehicles and how contractors conduct themselves.

That suits how we already work. A written report after every visit is not a novelty here; it is the minimum a property manager or estate manager should expect, and it is what makes it possible to keep track of a pool you are not personally standing next to. Salt air does the rest of the work of making this a demanding place to keep equipment alive.

Local know-how

What island pools need

1

Salt air, constantly

Oceanfront and near-oceanfront equipment corrodes years early. The pad gets checked and photographed every visit, not seasonally.

2

Town rules and scheduling

Palm Beach regulates how and when contractors work. We schedule to it rather than treating it as an inconvenience.

3

Reporting a manager can use

Estate and property managers need a record, not reassurance. Readings, dosages, tasks and photographs after every visit, in writing.

Questions

Do you work with property and estate managers?

Yes, and the reporting is built for it. Every visit produces a written record of readings, dosages, tasks completed and photographs, which is what a manager needs rather than a verbal assurance.

Can you work within the town's contractor rules?

Yes. Palm Beach sets requirements around service hours and vehicles. Tell us the property's requirements at the quote and we schedule to them from the first visit.

How much is pool service in Palm Beach?

Plans start at $129 a month, and every account is subject to a price quote by a technician or at the point of sale. Island properties sit well above the entry rate.
