Pools in Highland Beach

Salt air is the whole job

Highland Beach is a mile-wide strip of ocean, condos and single-family homes, and every pool on it is dealing with salt air all day. It's the most demanding town we service, and the one where the difference between a good pool company and a cheap one shows up fastest, usually on the equipment pad.

Chemistry here needs a firmer hand. Wind carries salt and fine sand into the water constantly, evaporation runs high, and pools sit open to the sky with no screen to slow any of it down. We test tighter, dose more often, and check the pad for corrosion on every visit rather than at a seasonal service. On oceanfront accounts we photograph the equipment as well as the water, so you have a record of how it's holding up.

Local know-how

What Highland Beach pools need

1

Equipment corrosion

Heaters, pump housings and light rings corrode fast this close to the water. We photograph the pad on the report so you can see it degrading rather than hearing about it once it fails.

2

Constant salt and sand load

Open pools this near the beach take on wind-blown salt and sand year round. That moves chemistry between visits, so we test rather than assume.

3

Condo and building access

Several Highland Beach properties need scheduled access and building coordination. We work to the building's rules and arrive in the window rather than whenever suits the day.
