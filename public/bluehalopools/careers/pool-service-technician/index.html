Pool Service Technician II

Run one of our established residential routes across Lake Worth, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach and Boca Raton. Two weeks of paid onboarding, then the route is yours.

The route

What you'll do

  • Service 12–17 residential pools a day, Monday through Friday
  • Test and balance water; brush, vacuum, net and empty baskets
  • Clean filters on schedule and log every visit in our route app
  • Inspect equipment and write up anything failing so the repair can be quoted
  • Flag add-on work and talk with homeowners professionally when they're home

Equipment repair and replacement are not part of this role. You catch problems, document them and hand them off.

Who we're looking for

What you bring

  • 2+ yearsrunning a residential pool route
  • Working command of pool chemistryand able to clear algae, cloudy water and staining on your own
  • Can identify pumps, filters, heaters and automationand describe what's failing
  • Valid Florida driver's licenseand a clean driving record
  • Authorized to work in the U.S.and able to provide a W-9

Nice to have: your own truck insured for business use (a company vehicle is available for top candidates), a CPO certification (or we pay for it), and saltwater, variable-speed pump or equipment repair experience.

What we provide

Built so you can focus on the pools

  • Your truck, our suppliesStart and end at home. We cover route fuel with a weekly allowance and supply the chemicals and equipment.
  • Tighter routesStops are sequenced to cut drive time, and we keep refining them so your day gets shorter.
  • Direct line to the ownerNo dispatcher and no call center. You work with the person who built the route.
  • An app for the paperworkStops and directions laid out for you. Log chemicals and notes at each pool, with no end-of-day catch-up.
