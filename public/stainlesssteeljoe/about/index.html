About Stainless Steel Joe, Inc.

Stainless Steel Joe is a unique metal shop with a lifetime of hands-on experience.

The owner Joseph started metal work in high school, vocational school in Camden County N.J. Areas of study were, Tig, Mig, Arc, Gas welding, metallurgy, and blueprint reading. Testing on various welding positions such as, vertical, overhead, and horizontal, were completed in order to receive his Certification as a welder.

Joseph has worked for years in the South Florida Marine welding industry for: Director & Gunnell in Dania Beach Florida, Dennison Marine also in Dania Beach, and Brady Marine out of Newark New Jersey. While working in the Marine welding industry there was an opportunity to weld and fit aluminum, Stainless Steel, and Steel, on ships, and yachts.

The yachting industry opened the door to decorative metals and stainless steel work. The level of quality in the yachting industry really brought out the design and fabrication skills Joseph needed in order to be successful.

It wasn’t until after a lot of encouragement from Joe’s close friends did Stainless Steel Joe become a company. My primary focus is on, Stainless Steel decorative metals, metal art, fabrication and Design.

No rules just an idea, a vision being formed to produce something awesome.

I have always had a passion to make things out of metal, mainly because I like the look and the strength that is represents. Metal work takes a lot of energy due to the nature of the materials. From raw materials to finished product is a journey, especially when doing metal art. The decision to go from industrial fabrication to metal art was a natural progression for me as I was always creative as a kid. Having the opportunity to work on several large public art projects seeing the creative process really inspired me to go full time as a metal artist.

I believe were all made to do great things and to follow your passions in life. I also believe helping others achieve their dreams is very important to your success in life.

Sincerely,
Joseph Panico
