GIANT MOTORS is a family owned Miami Beach institution which has been satisfying customers for over 50 years with a full spectrum of quality service and experience involving foreign and domestic vehicles. We are always making strides in our operation by purchasing state-of-the-art equipment. Our technicians have completed extensive training and have hard earned expertise in modern repair technology. We believe in doing things right and always satisfying the customer. That's why the business has grown and prospered for so many years.

We are open Monday through Friday from 8am to 5pm, but we have towing available 24 hours a day. We are located just minutes away at 470 NW 5th ST in Miami.

Every operation must meet our demanding standards of quality. Our state-of-the-art repair techniques assure that you receive the highest quality of bodywork.

In addition to repairing cars for people who come to us by word of mouth from satisfied customers, we are the recommended direct repair facility for several insurance companies and approved by all. We can handle your claim quickly, efficiently, and hassle free. Giant Motors, customer service that is second to none because you deserve it.

Your vehicle is one of your most important investments. We'll take care of it as if it were our own. Every effort is made to complete your repairs as quickly as possible. Our guarantee to you is to restore your vehicle to pre-crash condition. The best indication of our quality is the finished product.

We will handle towing and your insurance claims promptly, and help you make arrangements for a rental car. And did you know that Giant Motors offers a FREE car wash with every job? At Giant Motors, excellence is not an option; it's a requirement.

Integrity is the difference between a car that performs like new and one that just looks like it. Though we meticulously restore your vehicle's original factory appearance, we are committed to delivering more than just a cosmetic makeover. We'll repair your vehicle so it once again meets factory specifications.
