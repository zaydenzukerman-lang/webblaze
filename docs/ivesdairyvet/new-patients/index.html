Welcome — we're so glad you're here.

A few simple things to know before your first visit, so you and your pet feel right at home.

Book your visit

Call us at (305) 653-3939 or request an appointment online. Tell us a little about your pet and what's bringing you in.

Bring their history

If you can, bring any past vaccine or medical records — and a list of questions. No records? No problem, we'll help you track them down.

Meet your team

Expect an unhurried exam, a plan explained in plain English, and every question answered. You'll leave knowing exactly what your pet needs.

Manage your pet's care from your phone with the PetCare by DaySmart app — book appointments, request prescription refills, and keep your pet's records handy, all in one place. It's free on iOS and Android.
