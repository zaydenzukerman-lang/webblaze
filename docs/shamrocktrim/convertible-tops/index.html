Convertible Tops

Shamrock can replace your convertible top or broken rear glass.

We're comfortable working with an early classic or your brand new vehicle.

We currently work with over twenty-five new car dealers on warranty top repairs for current models. Miami is a great place to enjoy the sun, however, it dramatically reduces the life span of the convertible top material. Shamrock always uses the best available tops on the market.

We can install an original equipment top or use an aftermarket top that uses comparable material. Shamrock also repairs top frames, cylinders, hydraulic lines, and pumps. We also accept insurance claims and give written estimates.
