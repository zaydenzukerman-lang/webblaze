A word on shrimp

The problem with shrimp is that people go crazy for it, and one person can eat a pound of shrimp easy enough and will! So you have to make it difficult to just pick the shrimp out (which they will). Shrimp and grits are by far one of my favorite dishes and a signature, being a Lowcountry “Charleston” Chef/food historian. The shrimp shells and heads are used for the rich stock, and my grits are true speckled heart grits (I brokered grits for 10 years for a dozen mills across the South). I have 5 mills local that process grits when you order them, true dried sweet corn white or yellow, not tasteless hominy grits you get in the store. The Tasso Ham can be left out for meatless needs, or we make two batches: one meatless but seasoned with lemon-thyme, oregano and sage, and one with wonderful Tasso Ham (a seasoning pork from Creole Louisiana).
