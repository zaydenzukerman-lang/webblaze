Protect your new liner

Armor Shield & Perfect Pool Bottom

Armor Shield Liner Protection System — Armor Shield Floor Pad provides an important protective layer between a vinyl swimming pool liner and its sand base. Unlike traditional foam padding, Armor Shield is a polypropylene geotextile material that prevents cuts and punctures caused by rocks, roots, grass and even glass. It adds a layer of cushioning that helps eliminate footprints, resists acids, alkalis, salt, oils, mold and mildew, and installs easily with no wrinkles.

The Perfect Pool Bottom — a hard, flat, impervious polymer material placed under your pool to form a smooth bottom and protect the liner. It installs quickly once the site is prepared. Rocks, stones, nut grass and other objects below your pool can puncture an unprotected liner and cause leaks; this system helps eliminate that problem.
