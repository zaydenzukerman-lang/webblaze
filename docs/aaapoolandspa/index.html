Our staff

Who comes to your pool

No subcontractors. Background-checked, certified technicians in GPS-tracked trucks.

Our own employees

Our maintenance technicians are employed by us. They are not subcontractors.

Background checked

Before being hired, our employees submit to a criminal background check and driving record check. They are trained to work on the job site and they must demonstrate skills and knowledge.

Certified

Our pool technicians are CPO, Certified Pool and Spa Operators, by the National Swimming Pool Foundation (NSPF) and Certified Public Pool Service Technicians by the Miami Dade Health Department.

GPS-tracked trucks

All our company trucks are equipped with a GPS Tracking system to ensure your pool was visited and the time of the visit. This report is available to the customer at the time they request it.

Insured & bonded

Our employees are under worker's compensation insurance; our company has General Liability insurance and a Dishonesty Bond for your peace of mind.
