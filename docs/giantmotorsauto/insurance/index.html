Insurance

Approved and preferred by all insurance companies.

Giant Motors is approved and preferred by all insurance companies to make your collision repair experience as hassle-free as possible.

We have chosen to work very closely with most major insurance companies in an effort to help negotiate the best possible service in order to make sure that your car is returned to its Pre-accident condition. We are dedicated to using “new” original parts whenever they are available within your insurance coverage, and to provide you with easy-to-understand options when this is not possible. Giant Motors is dedicated to helping you achieve the best collision repair service possible.

Repair Authorization Form

Thank you for selecting Giant Motors to repair your vehicle.

For liability purposes, our insurance company requires that we obtain your signed authorization allowing us to have your vehicle in our lot, and to begin the estimating process as soon as possible. Here's how:

  1. Open the form. Giant Motors Authorization Form (PDF)
  2. Print and complete the information located at the top of the form.
  3. Sign and date the form at the bottom.
  4. Fax it to Giant Motors at (305) 531-5772.

If you have any questions, please feel free to give us a call. We look forward to working with you and your insurance company to make your collision repair experience as pleasant as possible.
