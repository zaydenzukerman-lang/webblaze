Plans

Compare your options and find the right fit for your needs.

Should I choose a private PPO or an ACA Marketplace plan?

The right option depends on your income, health history, doctors, prescriptions, and coverage priorities. Marketplace plans may include income-based subsidies and guaranteed eligibility, while private PPO options may offer broader provider access for applicants who qualify medically. We compare both based on your specific situation.

Do you only sell one company's plans?

No — we're an independent brokerage and compare plans across multiple carriers, including regional Blue-affiliated plans, national PPO carriers, integrated HMO carriers, and ACA Marketplace-focused carriers, so the recommendation is based on fit, not a quota.

Coverage

Understand your network, doctors, and what's actually covered.

I'm losing my job — how fast can new coverage start?

Job loss is a qualifying life event that opens a 60-day Special Enrollment Period for Marketplace plans. Private PPO plans aren't tied to that same monthly start-date rule and can often begin sooner — frequently faster than waiting on a COBRA election.

Can I keep my current doctor?

Provider participation varies by plan and network, so we always verify your specific doctors and hospitals against a plan's actual network before you enroll — never assume access based on plan type alone.

Small Business

Explore options for you and your team.

What's the difference between a group plan, ICHRA, and QSEHRA?

A traditional group plan is selected by the employer for everyone; an ICHRA lets you reimburse employees tax-free for individual plans they choose themselves with no minimum group size; a QSEHRA is a simpler version of the same idea for businesses under 50 employees. We'll help you pick based on team size and budget.

Getting Started

Find out when and how to get started, and where to get help.

Is this service actually free?

Yes. Licensed brokers are compensated by the insurance carrier at enrollment, not by you, so comparing plans and enrolling through us costs nothing extra.

How long does a consultation take?

Most first calls run 20-30 minutes. We'll ask about your household, budget, and current coverage, then walk through 2-3 plan options that fit — no pressure to decide on the spot.

Other Coverage

Medicare, Medicaid, and situations outside what we handle directly.

I'm turning 65 soon — can you help with Medicare?

We specialize in private, self-employed, and small-business coverage for working-age adults and do not handle Medicare enrollment. If you're approaching 65, we recommend contacting a Medicare-specific broker or 1-800-MEDICARE directly.

Do you help with Medicaid applications?

We don't process Medicaid applications. If your household likely qualifies for Medicaid based on income, your state Medicaid office or healthcare.gov is the right starting point — we're happy to point you in the right direction.
