What to know about coverage here

Florida uses HealthCare.gov, and Open Enrollment normally runs November 1 to January 15. Plan choices and provider networks differ between Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach, so we check your doctors and nearby hospitals for your county before you choose.

Florida hasn't expanded Medicaid, and the state has its own rule letting some young adults stay on a parent's insured plan until 30. See our Florida page for details.
