Oklahoma is mid-transition to its own Marketplace

Oklahoma moved to a state-based exchange built on the federal platform starting May 2026, with a fully independent state exchange planned for the 2028 plan year. For now, Oklahomans still enroll through HealthCare.gov.

Leaving a job at a smaller company

Oklahoma has a continuation law for employers with fewer than 20 employees. The exact continuation period depends on your policy — we'll pull the specifics for your situation.
