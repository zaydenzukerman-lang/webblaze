FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Why is it only $300 a year?+

We're lean, with no agency overhead — and we'd rather earn your trust with a fair price than oversell you. The $300 covers your whole site for the year: hosting, updates, and reports. Most clients grow with us from there.

What does the $300 a year include?+

Everything to keep your site live and current: hosting, unlimited small updates whenever you need them, a fast mobile-friendly build, and a simple monthly report on your traffic. No hidden fees, no contract — cancel anytime.

What if I want changes after it's live?+

Small updates — a new number, a price change, a fresh photo — are free anytime, included in your yearly fee. Just ask. Larger redesigns are quoted separately.

Do you help me get more customers, not just a site?+

Yes — that's the real value. Once your site's live, we can get you ranking higher on Google Maps and pulling in more reviews so more local customers actually find you. It's a separate monthly service, never bundled or pushy.
