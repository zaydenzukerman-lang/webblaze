Our history

Family owned and operated since 1972

Family owned and operated since 1972, Universal Tile Group prides itself as a direct importer of high quality tile at competitive pricing. The company began as a small showroom in Miami Beach, the first American venture of Hugo Ignorato, the founder of UTG. Born and raised in Naples, Italy, and a proud father of four, Hugo was a pioneer in the tile industry who labored countless late nights to ensure the success of his company.

In the mid-1980s, Hugo’s children — Gaetano, Luigi, and Angelica — proudly carried on their father’s legacy by expanding the expertise of the company into installations and home renovations. Since then, the company has taken on countless commercial and residential projects and has developed a presence as one of the most reputable flooring companies in South Florida.

For over 50 years, Universal Tile Group has served the needs of customers all over Florida by offering high-quality tile, in-house installation, and several other services that you sometimes won’t find at big-box retail stores. As the highest-rated tile and renovation company in South Florida, we abide by the highest industry standards — from corporate ethics to sustainability practices — and our commitment to our customers stands strongly: providing quality products and services customers can rely on for maximum value and satisfaction.
