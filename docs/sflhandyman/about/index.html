About

Helping others is the principle my business was founded on

SFL HANDYMAN offers quality handyman services to portions of Broward and Palm Beach County, Florida and is owned and operated by me, Billy Szatkiewicz.

My business was started when I decided to take my years of home improvement skills combined with my background in electrical engineering and offer them to members of the community. Helping others is the principle my business was founded on. I look forward for the opportunity to earn your business and trust.

Background

I have over 15 years of home improvement and maintenance experience. That combined with my Bachelor of Science Degree in Electronics Engineering and over 15 years experience working as a professional power electronics engineer gives me a unique set of skills and problem solving capabilities that allows me to tackle just about anything.

Why Choose Me?

Have you ever thought about how nice it would be to find a home repair expert that you trust with all of your home repairs and improvements? A person that you trust around your family and belongings?
