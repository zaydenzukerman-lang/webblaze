Our story

More than 30 years of mouthwatering meals

Chef Eric's Best has been preparing mouthwatering meals for more than 30 years.

Now available for purchase are our Sauces and Seasonings, for you to use in your own delicious dishes. Chef Eric's Best Foods Seasonings and Sauces are excellent for Jerk Lobster tail, Shrimp, Salmon, Chicken, Pork, and Steak.

Also available are delicious Caribbean candies and snacks like Busta Candy & Tamarind Balls, Solomon Gundy & Peppered Shrimp.

Chef Eric has captured the true 'jerk' flavor in his seasonings. The 'jerk' flavor comes through, cooked on the stove or on the grill (without the need for pimento wood).
