Jerk Seasoning

Chef Eric's Jerk Seasoning is ideal for seasoning any type of meat, including chicken, steak, and fish. Chef Eric has captured the true 'jerk' flavor in his seasonings. The 'jerk' flavor comes through, cooked on the stove or on the grill (without the need for pimento wood).

Ingredients & directions

Ingredients: Onion, scallions, scotch bonnet peppers, garlic, salt, thyme, soy sauce (soy beans, wheat, sugar, salt, potassium sorbate), pimento (allspice), citric acid & special spices.

Directions: Use about 2 tablespoons of Jerk Seasoning per pound of meat. Add a couple teaspoons of oil (canola oil is recommended, but olive oil or other cooking oils maybe used) and blend. Coat the meat with the seasoning blend and allow to marinate for at least 2 hours.
