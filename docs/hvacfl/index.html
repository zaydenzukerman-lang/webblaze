About us

40 years of HVAC products and customer service

Since 1985, HVAC Associates, Inc. has been one of the top HVAC manufacturers' representative firms serving the South Florida market with HVAC products.

In 2001, HVAC Associates expanded its operations to bring that same level of customer service and quality HVAC products to Central Florida.

Thanks to all of our HVAC customers and manufacturers for their many years of support!
