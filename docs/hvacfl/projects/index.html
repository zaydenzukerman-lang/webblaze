Waste Management Site

Pompano Beach, FL

This project is located at one of Waste Management’s many electric power CO-GEN plants. This particular plant is located in Pompano Beach, Florida and was designed as a methane gas recovery fired, steam turbine electric generating system, providing some 5 megawatts of power to the grid. Our cooling tower is an integral part of that system’s steam condenser water cooling cycle: circulating 4,750 GPM of 100°F entering water, leaving at 85°F, utilizing a type “CF 1900” fill. Nominally this would be considered around 3,000 tons of refrigeration cooling heat rejection.

Due to the nature of the process environment, several very interesting design conditions led the design engineers in the direction of fiberglass construction. Fiberglass is impervious to nearly every substance known to man, and is commonly used as a piping transporter of research laboratory liquid acid waste, safety encasement on gas station distribution piping, as well as storage tanks. It is also the material of choice in the marine industry. The cooling towers are rated for 150 MPH lateral wind weather environments and carry a 10 year warranty on the casing structure. The retrofitted steel cooling tower on this project did not withstand the rigors of the environment, or the heavy doses of chemicals required for the water quality a steam generating process of this type needs.

In short, this was the perfect marriage of equipment material composition with process environment requirements.
