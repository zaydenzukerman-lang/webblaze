Our story

Family-owned since 1994

Copy Graphics is your one stop shop for printing, signs and everything in between in Jacksonville Beach. Founded in 1994 by Susan and Allen Solomon, we are a family-owned, full service printer. We have an expert team with over 84 years of collective experience in the printing industry. This experience, combined with state of the art equipment, allows us to offer the highest quality of products and the best customer service while still offering the area’s most competitive prices.
