Maintenance agreements

The Importance of Preventive Air Conditioning Maintenance

Air conditioning systems in South Florida work hard for much of the year. Because many breakdowns can be prevented with routine maintenance — and a single emergency service call can cost more than a maintenance agreement — Prestige recommends having your system professionally maintained every six months, along with Florida Power & Light (FPL) and equipment manufacturers.

Benefits of a Prestige Maintenance Agreement

  • Priority scheduling
  • 10% discount on all repairs
  • Reduced diagnostic charges during normal business hours for residential customers
  • Transferable to another home in Palm Beach County
  • Lower operating costs
  • Fewer repairs
  • Extended equipment life

What's included in a maintenance visit

  • Clear drain line
  • Add algaecide to condensation drip pan
  • Test electrical connections and AMP draws
  • Check thermostat calibration
  • Clear debris from condenser coil (in place)
  • Clean evaporator coil (in place, if accessible)
  • Check relays, capacitors and contactor points
  • Test refrigerant levels for proper refrigerant charge
  • Check expansion valve
  • Inspect blower wheel
  • Lubricate necessary moving parts
  • Inspect AC unit wiring
  • Clean or replace air filters (filters not included)

Homeowners can help too: keep leaves, grass, ivy and other debris away from the outside unit, and check/replace your air filter once a month.

A/C copper thieves continually strike South Florida

Anti-Theft Security Cages

Copper theft is on the rise and leaves air conditioning units destroyed, requiring home and business owners to replace expensive equipment for a fraction of its copper scrap value. The average homeowner loss is $2,000–$6,000 — for commercial spaces with larger equipment, the cost can run $30,000 or more.

Protect your investment with a rugged, affordable A/C cage:

  • 16-gauge steel
  • Locks
  • Easy installation
  • Pleasing appearance
  • Removable for maintenance
  • Low cost
  • Fits almost every size A/C unit, commercial or residential

Sizes available from 34"H x 30"W x 30"D up to 59"H x 51"W x 51"D.
