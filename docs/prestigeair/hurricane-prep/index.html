Hurricane preparation

Prepare your central air conditioner for a hurricane

When preparing your home for a hurricane, don't forget about your central air conditioning system. High winds, flying debris, flooding and electrical power surges can cause serious damage to your equipment.

Call (561) 833-8861
1

Prior to hurricane season

Have your equipment inspected and maintenance performed to catch loose wires or safety hazards and confirm the system runs properly. Check that your outdoor unit is properly secured to the concrete slab — if not, it can become a 200 lb projectile in a storm. A hurricane-rated concrete slab with proper hurricane straps can withstand winds of approximately 170 MPH. Have a surge protector installed by your AC or electrical contractor — a good idea for your air conditioner and all major household appliances. Trim large tree branches close to your air conditioner, and secure loose items like patio furniture and children's toys that could become flying projectiles.

2

Several hours before the storm hits

Lower your thermostat a few degrees below normal to help with comfort during the storm.

3

Right before the storm

Turn the air conditioning system off at the circuit breaker to help prevent a power surge or electrical fire. Cover the outdoor unit with a tarp and bungee cord to help prevent flying debris from getting lodged inside the unit.

4

After the storm, once power is restored

Inspect your equipment for obvious signs of damage before turning the air conditioner back on. If there's visible damage, or you're in doubt as to whether your equipment is safe to operate, call a licensed contractor such as Prestige Air Conditioning Systems at (561) 833-8861.
