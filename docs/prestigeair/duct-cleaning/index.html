Duct cleaning services

Breathe easier with professionally cleaned air ducts

If your home is more than one year old, contaminants may be clinging to every inch of your duct system and flowing through the air you breathe. Our advanced method thoroughly scrubs and vacuums your ducts without harsh chemicals such as sealers.

Our Rotobrush method

The maze of vents and ducts in your heating/cooling system that are designed to make your home more comfortable may, in fact, be making you uncomfortable or even sick. You will be left with fresh, clean, virtually irritant-free air in your home or office.

We clean and sanitize your register covers to remove cooking oil, nicotine film and other dust-collecting residue. The air ducts are then brushed and vacuumed clean with our exclusive, patented Rotobrush, which removes allergy- and disease-causing contaminants that continually circulate throughout your air ducts.

The powerful yet flexible Rotobrush easily cleans all types of air duct construction — flex-ducts, round metal ducts, square metal ducts, fiberboard — including right-angle turns, reducers and multiple bends found in most duct systems. The Rotobrush vacuums as it sweeps the debris loose.
