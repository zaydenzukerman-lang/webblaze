FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Can you really get me to the top of Google Maps?

We get you found and climbing — we never promise a specific "#1," because rankings shift by who's searching and where, and nobody honest can guarantee a position. What we promise is the work (profile, reviews, website, citations) and a transparent monthly report on your real positions so you see the movement.

What's included in the $500/mo Google Growth?

Google Maps ranking work, full Google Business Profile management, our automated 5-star review system, website SEO, weekly Google posts, and a plain-English monthly ranking report. It's month-to-month — no long contract.

What does the $629 a year website include?

Everything to keep your site live and current: hosting, unlimited small updates whenever you need them, a fast mobile-friendly build, and a simple monthly report. No hidden fees, no contract — cancel anytime. And you never pay until your site is live and you've approved it.

How do the automated reviews work?

After a customer visits, our system sends them a friendly text or email asking for a Google review — one tap to leave it. It runs quietly in the background so your review count and star rating keep climbing without you chasing anyone. We reply to every review too.

Do you run Google or Facebook ads?

Yes — ads are an optional add-on for businesses that want to turn on demand quickly. We manage your Google and Meta ads; you pay the ad spend directly to the platform, and management is priced to your goals. It's never required — most clients start with ranking + reviews first.
