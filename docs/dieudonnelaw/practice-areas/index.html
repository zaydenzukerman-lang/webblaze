The Law Office of Dieudonne Cadet, P.A., realizes that being charged with a crime may be a frightening experience for you and we want to be there for you. Labor Law & Wage and Hour

We understand how hard you work and that you should be entitled to every penny that is legally yours. Fair pay and equal treatment in the workplace is the law and we do not believe in compromising those rights.

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), employees have rights that an employer cannot take away. Unfortunately, not all employers follow the FLSA. Whether employers are violating the FLSA intentionally or unintentionally, we feel that they should be held accountable for not paying their employees the wages they deserve. Although employees must generally be paid overtime for hours worked over forty in a workweek, many employers will stop at nothing to avoid complying with the law just so that profits may be increased. Companies have become increasingly aggressive in their efforts to cut costs at the expense of their employees. You have employment rights; let us protect those rights.

When you need legal help for improper compensation and discrimination in the workplace, we handle the following types of cases:

  • Unpaid Overtime Violations
  • Minimum Wage Violations
  • Wage & Hour Disputes
  • Wage & Hour Retaliation
  • Worker's Compensation
  • Worker's Compensation Retaliation
  • Unpaid Final Paychecks
  • Employment Contract Disputes
  • Unemployment Hearings
  • Employment Discrimination

Immigration Law

At the Law Office of Dieudonne Cadet, P.A., we want to assist you in achieving your immigration goals. Our practice is focused on helping people and their family members enter, live, and work in the country legally. We can guide you through the entire process of bringing your spouse, mother, father, sibling(s), minor child(ren), and adult child(ren) into the United States. Our clients trust us not only to prepare and file all necessary documents with the Department of Homeland Security, the Department of Labor, and the Department of State, but also to candidly advise them about constant changes in immigration law and policy and chances of successfully obtaining an immigration visa.

Our immigration services include:

  • Employment-Based Permanent Residence
  • Employment Authorization for Temporary Workers
  • U.S. Citizenship Petitions & Appeals
  • Waivers of Grounds of Inadmissibility
  • Temporary Protected Status
  • Advance Parole
  • Administrative Appeals
  • Criminal Alien Issues
  • Asylum
  • Appeals/Immigration Litigation
  • Other Temporary & Permanent Immigration Cases
