Lewis Electric is an equal opportunity employer.

Journeyman Electrician

You will be in charge of a 2 Person crew working in all facets of the Electrical Trade. Construction, Service and Troubleshooting skills a must. Will train properly motivated and licensed individual. Experience in Commercial / Industrial electrical installations, New Construction and Rework. Control knowledge a big plus.

Requirements

  • Current Dade County and Monroe County Journeyman's License, or able to meet the licensing requirements
  • Able to pass FDLE background check
  • Clean Florida drivers license
  • A common sense approach and a will to work

Benefits

Salary scaled with experience, plus overtime. Paid vacation, participation in Profit Sharing Plan, paid holidays, uniform allowance, health insurance plan, bonuses.

Electrician's Helper

You will be part of a 2 Person crew working in all facets of the Electrical Trade. Knowledge of materials and tools a must. Will train a properly motivated individual. Salary scaled with experience. Good benefits – Vacation, Health Insurance, Profit Sharing.

Requirements

  • Minimum of 2 years trade experience
  • Motivated to learn and to work
  • Able to pass FDLE background check for Burglar alarm division
  • Able to obtain a clean Florida Class D drivers license
  • Recent High School Graduate acceptable
