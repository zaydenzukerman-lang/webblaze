FAQ

StateShield Homeowner Questions

Clear answers, honest communication, and complete transparency.

What is StateShield?

StateShield is a proactive home maintenance membership designed to help homeowners understand, maintain, and improve the health of their home. Instead of waiting until something breaks, we help identify maintenance opportunities early, provide professional guidance, and help homeowners make smarter decisions.

Is StateShield a Home Warranty?

No. StateShield is not a home warranty and is not an insurance policy. We focus on prevention, education, maintenance, transparency, and helping homeowners avoid expensive surprises whenever possible.

What Do I Receive As a Member?

Annual Home Health Assessment, Home Health Score, Home Health Report, Priority Scheduling, Transparent Labor Pricing, StateShield Credits, and Access to Trusted Home Professionals. Additional benefits may vary depending on your membership plan.

What Is the Home Health Assessment?

A comprehensive visual inspection of your home's major systems and visible components, including roofing, windows, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, solar (if applicable), exterior, and safety.

What Is the Home Health Score?

A proprietary 0–100 scoring system that helps homeowners understand the overall visible condition of their home and track it year after year.

Will You Try to Sell Me Repairs?

No. Our technicians are trained to educate, not pressure. We explain what we find and provide transparent pricing. The decision is always yours.

Do I Have To Use StateShield For Repairs?

No. You are never required to hire StateShield. If you choose us, you'll receive the benefits available through your membership.

What Are StateShield Credits?

Every successful membership payment (Plus and Elite plans only) earns StateShield Credits. Credits accumulate while your membership remains active and can be applied toward eligible labor charges on qualifying services.

How Does Transparent Labor Pricing Work?

You'll receive a clear labor estimate before work begins. Materials are billed separately at cost whenever applicable, with documentation provided. No hidden labor charges. No surprise invoices.

How Long Does the Home Health Assessment Take?

Most assessments take approximately 45–60 minutes, depending on the size, age, and condition of the home.

Do I Need To Be Home?

Yes. An adult homeowner or authorized representative should be present so the technician can review findings and answer questions.

What Happens After the Assessment?

You'll receive your personalized Home Health Report including your Home Health Score, inspection photos, maintenance recommendations, priority items, estimated Labor Guide pricing, and available StateShield Credits.

Can I Cancel My Membership?

Yes. You may cancel according to your Membership Agreement. Our goal is to earn your business every year, not keep you because you're locked into a contract.

Why Should I Join Instead of Calling a Contractor Only When Something Breaks?

Waiting until something fails is usually the most expensive option. Preventative maintenance helps identify issues early and reduce unexpected repair costs.

Why Is StateShield Different?
