Family law mediation

Resolve it between yourselves.

The firm provides family law mediation services. Through mediation, parties may resolve a matter between themselves without a judge rendering the final decision and deciding for them.

In mediation, knowledge and understanding are key. With a degree in tax law in addition to her law degree, Ilene brings a focus and attention to the financial side inherent in divorce matters.
