A second home for your pet

Care that feels personal, because it is

We’re a small clinic on Ives Dairy Road that treats your pets the way we treat our own. From a puppy’s first exam to a senior dog’s golden years, Dr. Ahuja and our team take the time to know your pet — their history, their quirks, and what makes them feel safe.

Our veterinarian is Fear Free Certified, which means every visit is designed to lower stress for your pet, not just treat the problem. That’s what a second home should feel like.

Traveling with your pet?

Health Certificates and Pet Travel

Planning to travel with your pet? Our USDA-certified veterinarian will work with you to provide vaccines, testing, and health certificates for both domestic and international travel.

Please consult the USDA’s pet travel guidelines as early as possible to avoid any travel delays. Our team is here to make the process smooth, safe, and stress-free for you and your pet.

Your veterinarian

Dr. Aditi Ahuja, DVM

Dr. Ahuja earned her Doctorate of Veterinary Medicine from Cornell University, where she was inducted into the Phi Zeta Veterinary Honor Society. She completed a rotating internship in Small Animal Medicine and Surgery at Friendship Hospital for Animals in Washington, D.C., and has a passion for diagnosing and managing chronic diseases.

She is Fear Free Certified and an active member of the AVMA and the Veterinary Business Management Association — and she finds deep fulfillment in helping families understand their pets’ health at every step.
