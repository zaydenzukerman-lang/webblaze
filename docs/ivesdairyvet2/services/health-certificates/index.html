Health Certificates

Planning to travel with your pet? Our USDA-certified veterinarian will work with you to provide vaccines, testing, and health certificates for both domestic and international travel.

Travel paperwork we handle

  • Domestic health certificates
  • International health certificates
  • Travel vaccine requirements
  • Pre-travel testing
  • Microchip verification
  • USDA requirement guidance

What to expect

01

Start early

Please consult the USDA’s pet travel guidelines as early as possible to avoid any travel delays — some destinations need months of lead time.

02

Exam and paperwork

We examine your pet, bring vaccines and testing up to the destination’s requirements, and complete the certificate.

03

Travel with confidence

Our team is here to make the process smooth, safe, and stress-free for you and your pet.

One tip from our team

Every country — and every airline — has its own rules, and they change. The single best thing you can do is call us the moment you know your travel dates. We’ll map the timeline backwards from your departure so nothing is left to the last minute.
