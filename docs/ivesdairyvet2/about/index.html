Aditi Ahuja, DVM

Dr. Aditi Ahuja earned her Doctorate of Veterinary Medicine from Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine, where she focused on Small Animal Medicine and was inducted into the Phi Zeta Veterinary Honor Society. She is also Fear Free Certified and an active member of the AVMA and Veterinary Business Management Association (VBMA).

Dr. Ahuja has a passion for diagnosing and managing chronic diseases. She completed a rotating internship in Small Animal Medicine and Surgery at Friendship Hospital for Animals in Washington, D.C., gaining hands-on experience in emergency care and multiple specialties. While she once considered pursuing a residency in internal medicine, her true passion lay in guiding pet owners through the journey of early diagnosis, intervention, and lifelong care. She finds deep fulfillment in helping families understand their pets’ health and supporting them every step of the way.

Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her dogs, baking, and hosting board game nights.

How we work

Fear Free, and proud of it

A vet visit shouldn’t be the scariest day of your pet’s month. As a Fear Free Certified practice, we work to lower stress at every step — how we handle, how we schedule, even how the room feels — so pets come back happy to see us.

We serve pets in North Miami Beach, Aventura, Sunny Isles, Hallandale Beach, and anyone willing to make the drive.
